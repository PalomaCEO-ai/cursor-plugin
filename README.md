# PalomaCEO.ai for Business

Run your business on [PalomaCEO.ai](https://palomaceo.ai) from Grok Bot or Cursor: social media, your menu in Paloma, your website and ordering page, online ordering and delivery, and sales reports.

## What it does

- **Social media:** draft, schedule, move or cancel posts on the channels you connected in the PalomaCEO.ai app.
- **Menu in Paloma:** dishes, prices, categories and photos.
- **Website and ordering page:** pages, design, languages, business profile.
- **Online ordering and delivery:** settings, promo codes, gift cards.
- **Sales and reports:** read only.

The assistant reads and prepares; nothing changes until you tap. Every change waits in your Inbox in the PalomaCEO.ai app until you tap Approve there. It never changes your POS, never writes to your guests, never refunds or charges anyone and never sees your guests' names, phones or messages.

## Getting started

1. Install the plugin in Grok Bot ("Connect apps") or Cursor. The first time it is used, a PalomaCEO.ai window opens: sign in (or create an account), pick your business and the areas the assistant may work in.
2. Ask, for example: "How were sales this week compared with last week?", "Add borscht to the menu at $9", "Post our new dumplings to Facebook tomorrow at 11 AM".
3. Approve the change in the PalomaCEO.ai app.

You can see and disconnect it any time in the PalomaCEO.ai app under Connections.

## Data

The plugin connects the assistant to one server, PalomaCEO.ai's own `https://mcp.palomaceo.ai/business`, with the access you allow when you sign in. It sends that server your requests for your business (for example a post's text, a dish and its price, the dates of a report) and gets back your business's data for the areas you allowed. The plugin sends nothing anywhere else, and its files hold no passwords or keys. In both Grok Bot and Cursor the connection runs through Cursor's connector service (Anysphere, Inc.): it keeps the sign-in PalomaCEO.ai gives it until you disconnect, and your requests and what our server sends back pass through it. That content is also processed by the AI model behind the assistant: xAI's models in Grok Bot, the model you pick in Cursor. Each of those companies handles it under your own agreement with it, not under PalomaCEO.ai's Privacy Policy. How PalomaCEO.ai keeps and uses this data: https://palomaceo.ai/privacy
